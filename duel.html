Melee Duel

← Menu · Solo arena · Character builder

Two players, one screen. Movement is in the open; each turn the two players pick their actions in secret, one after the other, and the tablet is passed between them. Experience is awarded (30 for the winner, 20 for the loser) and added to each fighter's arena EP, but nobody can die for good and no win or loss is marked. Either player may escape through their own door (stand on one of your four entrance hexes at the start of your move).
