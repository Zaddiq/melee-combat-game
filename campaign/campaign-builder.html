For campaign play: a hero or wizard with talents, a purse and as much gear as the money allows. The talents, their costs and the prices are NOT part of this page: you paste them in once (the "Rules data" box) and they stay in this browser only. This is the only builder in the campaign game: the character open here is the one the Colosseum and the phones offer as "From the builder", and what a phone sends back lands here. To bring in a character from the old game, paste its data (button below).

Rules data
Paste your campaign data (JSON) and press Save. It never leaves this browser. Check every number against your own book: the data decides what a talent costs and what a sword sells for.

1. The character

Paste a character from the old game's builder (its "Copy data" button) or any character data, then press Use it.

2. Ready for the fight

Your gear has no count limit: money is the limit. For a fight you choose what is in your hands, slung on your back and sheathed. The Colosseum uses the right hand, the left hand, the first slung item and the first sheathed dagger for now (it does not know the other slots yet).

3. Use the character

The character on this page is saved automatically as the open character of this browser (once it has a name): the Colosseum's "Load from builder" and a phone's "From the builder" use it. The text below is for pasting somewhere else.

Magic items

Weapons, armor and shields with enchantments, taken from your Magic items list (make or change them there). A weapon's DX bonus is added to your adjusted DX whenever it is readied. A weapon that can flame does more damage while "Flame on" is ticked. Armor and shields stop extra hits. Other powers are in the item's note: the fight does not enforce them.

Loot

Things found that are worth money (gems, jewelry, trade goods). Sell them between sessions: the money goes into "Money to spend" above.
