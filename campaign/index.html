Melee Game

Tools for The Fantasy Trip: Melee and Wizard, using the Third Edition (2018) rules.

Host a campaign tableOpens the Colosseum with the phone table and your module, at the room where you left off. Campaign character builderThe campaign's only character builder: talents (with the IQ budget), a purse, gear, loot and magic items. The Colosseum and phones use the character open here. You paste your own rules data in; it stays in your browser. Old builder (to bring characters over)The original game's builder, outside the campaign. Open a character there, press "Copy data", come back here and use "Paste a character…" in the campaign builder. Nothing is changed in the old game. BestiaryA monster manual: stats, gear and notes for each enemy. Everything saved here can be picked as the computer side in the Colosseum. Magic itemsMagic swords, armor and shields with their enchantments, ready to give to a campaign hero. Follows the Advanced Wizard enchantment rules. Fight simulatorTwo fighters, 10,000 straight fights at range 1, with win chances and damage statistics. Arena ClassicFight a computer opponent on the arena map, earn experience, and take a fighter to 16 wins. Unlimited ArenaArena Classic plus spells, wizard opponents and summoned creatures. Bring a wizard or a fighter from the builder. Melee DuelTwo people, one tablet: fighters or wizards with spells, pick actions in secret and pass the screen back and forth. Earns a little XP, no permanent death. Fight a friend onlineTwo people in different places: you host here and play Player 1, your friend joins from their phone or computer with a table name. Secret orders, experience for both. ColosseumOne to four people: alone against the computer, or free for all and teams on one tablet or with phones; secret orders, experience for everyone. Join a table with your phoneYour host opens a table on the tablet: type its name here and make your secret picks on this phone. Blank character sheetA clean sheet to print for players to fill in by hand: attributes, a strength track, gear, spells, notes and experience.

Fighters you save in the builder and your arena records are kept in this browser only.
