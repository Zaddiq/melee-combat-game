Rules (Advanced Wizard, Weapon/Armor Enchantment): a weapon's DX bonus and damage bonus add up to at most 5, in any mix. Armor and shields stop 1 to 5 extra hits. An enchanted iron weapon or armor still spoils a wizard's casting: it has to be silver. A flaming weapon does +2 damage if it does 1 die or less, +3 if it does up to 2 dice, and one more die if it does more than 2 (a bow adds 1, a crossbow 2); it only flames for someone who knows how, so the Flame on tick is up to you. Anything else an item does (a command word, a ring's power) goes in the note and you apply it at the table.
