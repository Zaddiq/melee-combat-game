Melee Game

Tools for The Fantasy Trip: Melee and Wizard, using the Third Edition (2018) rules.

Character builderBuild a Wizard or Melee fighter, check adjusted DX for every gear setup, and print the sheet. Fight simulatorTwo fighters, 10,000 straight fights at range 1, with win chances and damage statistics. Arena ClassicFight a computer opponent on the arena map, earn experience, and take a fighter to 16 wins. Unlimited ArenaArena Classic plus spells, wizard opponents and summoned creatures. Bring a wizard or a fighter from the builder. Melee DuelTwo people, one tablet: fighters or wizards with spells, pick actions in secret and pass the screen back and forth. Earns a little XP, no permanent death. ColosseumTwo to four people, one tablet: free for all or teams, secret orders passed around the table, experience for everyone.

Fighters you save in the builder and your arena records are kept in this browser only.
