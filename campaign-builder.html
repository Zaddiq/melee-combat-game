For campaign play: a hero or wizard with talents, a purse and as much gear as the money allows. The talents, their costs and the prices are NOT part of this page: you paste them in once (the "Rules data" box) and they stay in this browser only. Start from a fighter or wizard you already made in the regular builder, add talents and buy gear here, then copy the result to the Colosseum.

Rules data
Paste your campaign data (JSON) and press Save. It never leaves this browser. Check every number against your own book: the data decides what a talent costs and what a sword sells for.

2. Ready for the fight

Your gear has no count limit: money is the limit. For a fight you choose what is in your hands, slung on your back and sheathed. The Colosseum uses the right hand, the left hand, the first slung item and the first sheathed dagger for now (it does not know the other slots yet).

3. Use the character

In the Colosseum, paste this text into a player's "paste" box. A phone can use it too if you make it the open character (second button) on that phone's browser.

Magic items

A magic weapon found in an adventure. Its DX bonus is added to your adjusted DX whenever it is readied (it counts for every DX roll in a fight). The damage bonus adds to its hits. Put anything else it does (a command word, flaming) in the note: the fight does not enforce it, you do at the table. It is carried on the character to the phones.
